CardioCare - Cardiology & Heart Health Landing Page

A modern, dark-themed medical landing page designed for a cardiology clinic. Built completely from scratch using pure, human-written HTML5 and CSS3 with a focus on clean Flexbox layouts and mobile responsiveness.

🌟 Features & Sections

Sub-Header & Navigation Bar: Quick access to 24/7 emergency contact info, working hours, navigation links, and an appointment booking CTA.

Hero Section: Bold headline, trust badges, clinic statistics (20+ Years Experience, 10K+ Patients Treated, 98% Satisfaction Rate), and consultation buttons.

Emergency Callout Banner: High-visibility red banner for immediate 24/7 cardiac support.

Online Appointment Booking Form: Clean, responsive form allowing patients to select a date, department, and describe their concerns.

Meet the Doctor: Profile showcase highlighting Dr. Jonathan Carter's credentials, certifications, and research background.

Comprehensive Medical Services: Grid of 6 specialized cardiology service cards.

Why Choose Us & Red Stats Banner: Key clinic differentiators paired with a full-width statistics highlight bar.

Health Blog & FAQ: Curated heart health articles and a clean Frequently Asked Questions list.

Newsletter & Contact Info: Email subscription box, clinic address, directions link, and operating hours.

Multi-Column Footer: Organized quick links and real-time clinic status indicator.

🛠️ Technologies Used

HTML5: Semantic structure (<header>, <section>, <nav>, <footer>, <form>).

CSS3: Custom styling using Flexbox, CSS linear gradients, custom properties, and @media (max-width: 768px) queries for mobile devices.

Font Awesome: Icons for medical badges, contact details, and UI buttons.
